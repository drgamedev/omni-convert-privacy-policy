# Privacy Policy for Omni Convert

**Effective Date:** October 15, 2026

Omni Convert is developed and published by **Healthy Mind Media**.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android application **Omni Convert** handles information.

## 1. Offline Operation

Omni Convert is designed primarily as an offline conversion and calculation application.

Its unit conversions, scientific calculations, favorites, and related calculator features operate locally on your device and do not require artificial intelligence services or runtime API calls.

The app does not include currency conversion or online exchange-rate functionality.

## 2. Information Stored by the App

Omni Convert may store app preferences and user-selected favorites locally on your device so that your preferred conversion tools can be accessed quickly.

Healthy Mind Media does not operate a server that receives or stores your conversion history, calculations, favorites, or other app content.

## 3. Personal Data

Healthy Mind Media does not directly collect, sell, rent, or store personal information through Omni Convert.

The app does not require you to create a Healthy Mind Media account.

## 4. Advertising

Omni Convert displays advertisements provided through **Google Mobile Ads / Google AdMob**.

Although Healthy Mind Media does not maintain its own database of personal information, Google and its advertising services may automatically process certain information when ads are displayed. Depending on your device, location, consent choices, and Google settings, this may include information such as:

- Device or advertising identifiers
- IP address
- General device information
- App interaction or advertising-related information
- Approximate location inferred from network information
- Diagnostic information used for ad delivery, fraud prevention, security, and measurement

Google may use this information to provide, measure, secure, and, where permitted, personalize advertisements.

The handling of information by Google is governed by Google's own privacy policies and advertising policies.

## 5. Personalized and Non-Personalized Ads

Depending on applicable law, your region, Google settings, and the consent options presented to you, Google may provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

Where consent is required, the app or Google's consent tools may ask you to make choices regarding advertising and privacy.

## 6. No Sale of Personal Information by Healthy Mind Media

Healthy Mind Media does not sell or rent personal information collected through Omni Convert.

Any information processed independently by Google for advertising is governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Omni Convert is a general-purpose utility and productivity application and is not specifically designed or directed toward children under 13.

Healthy Mind Media does not knowingly collect children's personal information on its own servers.

If the app is made available to age groups subject to special advertising requirements, advertising configuration will be handled in accordance with applicable Google Play and Google advertising policies.

## 8. Data Security

The app's locally stored preferences and favorites are protected by the security mechanisms provided by your Android device and operating system.

Information processed by Google's advertising services is subject to Google's own security practices and policies.

No electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

Healthy Mind Media does not maintain a server-side copy of your conversion activity, favorites, or calculations.

You can remove locally stored Omni Convert data by:

- Removing favorites or preferences within the app where available
- Clearing the app's storage through Android settings
- Uninstalling the application

Data independently processed by Google may be retained according to Google's own policies and your Google account or advertising settings.

## 10. Third-Party Services

Omni Convert may use Google services associated with advertising and app distribution, including:

- Google Mobile Ads / Google AdMob
- Google Play services
- Google Play Store

These services are governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features, advertising setup, data practices, or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page and the effective date will be revised.
